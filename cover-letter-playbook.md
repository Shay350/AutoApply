# Cover Letter Playbook (Template)

## Voice & Tone

- Voice: Confident, specific, builder-first
- Formality level: Professional but modern
- Energy level: Focused and clear
- Avoid:
  - Empty buzzwords
  - Overly generic praise
  - Unverifiable claims

---

## Positioning

- Core professional identity (1–2 lines):
  [Who you are professionally + what you build]

- Top 3 strengths:
  1. [Strength #1]
  2. [Strength #2]
  3. [Strength #3]

- Industries/domains of strongest fit:
  - [Domain 1]
  - [Domain 2]
  - [Domain 3]

- Preferred tech/tools (if relevant):
  - [Tech 1]
  - [Tech 2]
  - [Tech 3]

---

## Proof Points (Use Real Facts Only)

- Achievement 1 (impact + metric):
  [Project/work + measurable impact]

- Achievement 2 (impact + metric):
  [Project/work + measurable impact]

- Achievement 3 (impact + metric):
  [Project/work + measurable impact]

---

## Constraints

- Max length (words): 350
- Min length (words): 180
- Include salary mention? No
- Mention relocation? Only if relevant
- Mention visa/work auth? Only if explicitly required

---

## Reusable Building Blocks

### Opening line options

- I’m applying for the [Role Title] role because it aligns with the kind of systems I’ve been building and shipping.
- I’m excited about the opportunity to contribute to [Company Name] as a [Role Title].

### Value proposition options

- I build systems that are both technically solid and genuinely useful to end users.
- I’m comfortable owning work end-to-end, from implementation to production readiness.
- I move quickly while keeping quality and structure high.

### Closing line options

- I’d welcome the opportunity to contribute to your team.
- Thank you for your time and consideration.

---

## Customization Rules per Job

- Mirror 2–3 exact keywords from the job posting.
- Reference the company’s mission/product in one specific sentence.
- Tie one proof point directly to a listed requirement.
- Do not fabricate experience, titles, dates, or metrics.
- Keep paragraphs concise (2–3 sentences each).

---

## Output Format Preference

- Default: Plain text
- Alternative: Short email-style format
